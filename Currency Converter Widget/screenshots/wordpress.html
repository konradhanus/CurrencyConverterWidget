Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How often are exchange rates updated?

Rates are updated every time you open the app or tap the widget (limitations apply to save battery). We use the Frankfurt API which updates rates daily on weekdays.

Does the app work offline?

Yes! Once a rate is downloaded, it is saved on your device. You can convert currencies and track your budget even without an internet connection.

Is my financial data private?

Absolutely. All your expenses, budget history, and settings are stored only on your device. We do not have access to your data. See the Privacy Policy section below.

Why is the widget not updating?

iOS limits how often widgets can refresh to save battery. Opening the main app will force an immediate update of the rates in the widget.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 24, 2025

Currency Converter Widget ("the App") is designed to respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your rights regarding your information.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

The App operates primarily on your device. We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal details (such as name, email address, or phone number).

2. Local Data Storage

All user preferences and data created within the App are stored locally on your device using Apple's secure UserDefaults system. This includes:

  • Selected currencies (e.g., USD, PLN, EUR).
  • Budget settings (amounts, dates).
  • Expense history.

This data is stored within an "App Group" container to allow synchronization between the main App and the Widget. This data never leaves your device's secure environment.

3. Third-Party Services & Network Requests

To provide up-to-date currency exchange rates, the App makes network requests to a third-party API:

  • Service Provider: Frankfurter API.
  • Data Transmitted: Only the currency codes (e.g., "from=USD&to=EUR") are sent to the API.
  • Privacy: No personal user data is sent. However, like any web request, your IP address may be visible to the API provider for technical connectivity purposes.

4. Security (Guideline 1.5)

We take the security of your data seriously:

  • Apple Sandbox: The App runs within the standard iOS/macOS sandbox environment, preventing unauthorized access to other files or data on your device.
  • HTTPS: All network communication with the exchange rate API is encrypted using HTTPS (TLS/SSL).
  • No Remote Servers: We (the developers) do not operate a backend server that stores your usage data.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from or market to children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any personal data, the App is safe for users of all ages.
